How to Play

  • You start with 100 pixels as currency.
  • Tap a pill in the shop to buy it. It goes into your chamber.
  • The first item in the chamber is your next shot. Tap other slots to reorder.
  • Pull back on the launcher (right side) and release to fire.
  • Pull further = more power. Direction follows your pull.
  • If chamber is empty, you fire a default 1-pixel shot for free.
  • Tap the canvas while a shot is flying to detonate early.
  • Destroying PFP pixels earns pixels (yellow +N).
  • Destroying red obstacles costs pixels (red -N).
  • Gravity: When pixels are destroyed, pixels above will fall!

Pill Types

  • Default: 1 pixel (free when no ammo)
  • Spread pills: Fire multiple pixels in a spread pattern
  • Higher-tier pills fire more pixels: 10, 20, 40, 80, 160, 320, 640
