How to Play

  • You start with a bank of 100 pixels.
  • Tap a bomb in the shop to buy it (cost in pixels).
  • Drag back from the launcher handle to set angle & power, then release to fire.
  • Tap while a bomb is flying to detonate early.
  • Destroying PFP pixels adds pixels and score (yellow +N).
  • Destroying red blocking pixels subtracts pixels and score (red –N).
  • Getting pixels into the lava also counts as destroyed.
