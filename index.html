How to Play

  • You start with 100 pixels as currency.
  • Tap a bullet in the shop to buy it. It goes into your chamber.
  • The first item in the chamber is your next shot. Tap other slots to reorder.
  • Pull back on the launcher and release to fire.
  • Use the arrow buttons to move the launcher up/down.
  • Pull further = more power. Direction follows your pull.
  • Tap canvas to detonate Bombs/Mega early (Basic and Spread cannot be detonated).
  • Destroying PFP pixels earns pixels (yellow +N).
  • Destroying red obstacles costs pixels (red -N).
  • Later levels have moving targets!

Bullet Types

  • Basic: 10 pixels - Simple shot
  • Bomb: 20 pixels - Detonatable!
  • Spread: 25 pixels - Multiple shots (unlocks at level 3)
  • Mega: 40 pixels - Big explosion, Detonatable! (unlocks at level 5)
