How to Play

  • You start with a bank of 100 pixels.
  • Tap a bomb in the blue shop to buy it (cost in pixels). Purchases go into the chamber queue.
  • The first item in the chamber is the next shot. Click any slot to swap it with the first and reorder.
  • Move the launcher up/down on the thin vertical track at the right to change your angle.
  • Click/hold the colored pixel on the power bar to let power oscillate, or tap - / + to nudge power down/up.
  • Press the Fire button (or double-click the pixel on the power bar) to shoot left with the current power.
  • Tap the game area while a bomb is flying to detonate early.
  • Destroying PFP pixels adds pixels and score (yellow +N).
  • Destroying red blocking pixels subtracts pixels and score (red –N).
